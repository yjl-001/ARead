# ARead Product Intro Design

## Style Prompt
A quiet, intelligent product intro for a reading and research workspace. The look should feel focused, editorial, and modern: a soft dark canvas, crisp typography, subtle glass and paper-light overlays, and calm motion that lets the background video remain the hero.

## Colors
- `#0A0F14` deep ink background
- `#F7F4EA` warm paper title
- `#89B4FF` focused blue accent
- `#D7E7C6` soft highlight accent
- `rgba(10, 15, 20, 0.56)` cinematic overlay

## Typography
- Primary: `Inter`, `Avenir Next`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Use large, airy title type with normal letter spacing and compact supporting copy.

## What NOT To Do
- Do not use loud gradients or decorative blobs.
- Do not obscure the background video with heavy cards.
- Do not use fast, bouncy, or chaotic motion.
- Do not crowd the title area with feature explanations.
